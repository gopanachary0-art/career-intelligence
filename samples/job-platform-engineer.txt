Job #2 — Platform Engineer
Company: Orbital Freight
Location: Hybrid Berlin · Full-time

ABOUT THE ROLE
Orbital Freight is building an internal developer platform so product teams can ship services without reinventing CI, observability, and deployment. As a Platform Engineer you will own paved roads for Kubernetes workloads and golden-path templates.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Build and maintain internal platform tooling (CLI, templates, GitHub Actions workflows).
- Operate Kubernetes clusters and service mesh basics.
- Define SLOs and improve deployment safety (canaries, rollbacks).
- Partner with product squads to reduce time-to-first-deploy.
- Write clear docs and run enablement sessions.

REQUIREMENTS
- 4+ years in platform, SRE, or backend infrastructure roles.
- Hands-on Kubernetes (deployments, networking, RBAC).
- Strong Linux fundamentals and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform).
- Experience with observability stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry).
- Comfortable coding in Go or Python for tooling.
- Incident management experience.

PREFERRED
- AWS EKS / GCP GKE at scale.
- Policy-as-code (OPA/Kyverno).
- Prior experience migrating monoliths to services.
- Familiarity with developer experience metrics.

NICE TO HAVE
- Contributions to open-source platform tooling.
- Security certifications.
