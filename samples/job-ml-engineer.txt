Job #3 — Machine Learning Engineer
Company: Helix Insights
Location: Remote · Full-time

ABOUT THE ROLE
Helix Insights builds forecasting models for retail supply chains. We need an ML Engineer to take models from notebook to production: training pipelines, feature stores, and online inference.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Train and evaluate supervised models (time-series and tabular).
- Build reproducible training pipelines (Airflow/Kubeflow or similar).
- Deploy real-time and batch inference services.
- Partner with data scientists on experimentation and model monitoring.
- Improve data quality checks and feature documentation.

REQUIREMENTS
- 3+ years applying ML in production environments.
- Strong Python and familiarity with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Experience with feature engineering and offline evaluation metrics.
- Comfortable with SQL and large analytical datasets.
- Understanding of model drift, monitoring, and rollback strategies.

PREFERRED
- MLOps tooling (MLflow, Feast, SageMaker, Vertex AI).
- Experience with LLM fine-tuning or RAG systems is a plus but not the core of this role.
- Spark or BigQuery experience.
- Published papers or public technical writing.

NOTE
This role emphasizes classical ML and MLOps more than application engineering.
