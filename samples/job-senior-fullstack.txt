Job #1 — Senior Full Stack Engineer
Company: Contour Health
Location: Remote (EU) · Full-time

ABOUT THE ROLE
Contour Health is hiring a Senior Full Stack Engineer to own patient-facing workflows in our care coordination product. You will ship features across React/Next.js and Node.js services, improve reliability, and collaborate closely with clinicians and designers.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design and implement end-to-end features in TypeScript (Next.js + Node APIs).
- Model relational data in PostgreSQL and evolve schemas safely.
- Improve observability: structured logs, metrics, and traces.
- Participate in on-call and postmortems.
- Mentor engineers and contribute to technical RFCs.

REQUIREMENTS
- 5+ years professional software engineering experience.
- Strong TypeScript, React, and Node.js.
- Solid PostgreSQL skills (indexes, transactions, migrations).
- Experience shipping production web applications.
- Comfortable with Docker and CI/CD.
- Excellent written communication.

PREFERRED
- Next.js App Router experience.
- Healthcare or other regulated domain experience.
- Exposure to RAG / LLM features in product.
- AWS experience (ECS/EKS, RDS, S3).

BENEFITS
Competitive salary, learning budget, flexible hours, equity.
